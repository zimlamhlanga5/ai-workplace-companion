Objective

Create a single integrated productivity platform that improves workplace efficiency through AI-powered tools. The application should demonstrate practical AI implementation, prompt engineering, professional UI/UX design, and responsible AI usage.

Core Features
1. Smart Email Generator

Allow users to generate professional emails based on their requirements.

Inputs:

Recipient name
Email purpose
Key information
Tone selection (Formal, Friendly, Professional, Persuasive)

Outputs:

Professional email draft
Suggested subject line
Editable text area
Copy and regenerate buttons
2. Meeting Notes Summarizer

Enable users to paste meeting notes and receive concise summaries.

Outputs:

Executive summary
Key discussion points
Decisions made
Action items
Deadlines
Follow-up recommendations
3. AI Task Planner

Help users organize and prioritize work.

Inputs:

Task list
Priority levels
Deadlines

Outputs:

Daily schedule
Weekly planner
Priority ranking
Productivity recommendations
4. AI Research Assistant

Assist users with workplace research and information gathering.

Inputs:

Research topic
Question or prompt

Outputs:

Topic summary
Key insights
Recommendations
Advantages and disadvantages
Suggested next steps
5. AI Chatbot Interface

Provide an interactive workplace assistant.

Capabilities:

Answer workplace questions
Generate ideas
Summarize information
Assist with writing tasks
Support productivity planning

AI Prompt Engineering

Use structured prompts behind each feature.

Examples:

Email Generator:
"You are a professional business communication expert. Generate a clear, concise, and professional email based on the provided information and selected tone."

Meeting Summarizer:
"You are an executive assistant. Summarize the meeting notes and extract decisions, action items, responsibilities, and deadlines."

Task Planner:
"You are a productivity coach. Organize tasks according to urgency, importance, and deadlines."

Research Assistant:
"You are a research analyst. Provide a concise summary, insights, recommendations, and practical conclusions."
